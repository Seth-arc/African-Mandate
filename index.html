01 // The Situation

State of Délitement

The ink on the map no longer reflects the ground. Insurgencies control the trade routes. Climate erodes the arable land. The state is retreating, leaving a three-million-square-kilometer vacuum.

02 // The Players

Transactional Power

Transitional governments demand legitimacy. Non-state actors control the resources. Foreign proxies fill the voids left by retreating Western forces.

03 // The Stakes

Collateral Geography

Policy failures compound into humanitarian collapse. 11 million civilians are caught between expanding deserts and expanding conflicts. If the Sahel breaks, the shockwaves will not respect international borders.

04 // The Strategy

The Limits of Force

Kinetic operations secure territory, but they cannot govern it. You cannot airstrike a drought. Stability will require unpalatable compromises across security, economics, and climate policy.

Briefing // Experience Overview

African Mandate:
Sahel Arena

A geopolitical simulation of state fragility and asymmetric crisis. You have been appointed as the African Union Special Envoy to the Sahel. Your mandate is stabilization; your reality is a compounding cycle of climate collapse, insurgent expansion, and military coups.

You must negotiate with transitional juntas, manage non-state actors, and allocate severely limited resources across a deteriorating landscape. You cannot solve every problem. You must choose which crises to mitigate, which factions to leverage, and which compromises to swallow.

11 million civilians are collateral to your policies. The continent's security architecture rests on what you build—or what you allow to burn.
