# AI GENERATION PROMPTS: African Mandate - Sahel Arena

This document contains specific prompts for generating the defined media assets using **Google Veo** (for video) and **Midjourney** (for static images).

---

## 1. Google Veo Prompts (Video Assets)

These prompts are designed for high-resolution, photorealistic cinematic generation.
**Unified Aesthetic**: Afro-Futurist Political Thriller meets National Geographic. Anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, slow deliberate camera movement, film grain.
**Color Palette**: High contrast. Golden hour warmth (oranges/yellows) for land, cool high-tech blues/teals for UI/Command, desaturated gritty tones for crisis.

### 1.1 Opening Sequence (Cinematics)

*   **SEQ_01_THE_LAND**
    *   **Prompt**: Cinematic drone shot of the Sahara desert dunes at golden hour, transitioning smoothly into the semi-arid Sahel savanna with scattered acacia trees. The landscape then morphs into verdant farmland near a river. Finally, a time-lapse effect shows the green land turning dry and yellow, depicting rapid desertification. Shot on anamorphic lens, 4k, National Geographic aesthetic. High contrast lighting, deep orange and gold hues turning to desaturated dust. Slow, majestic camera movement.

*   **SEQ_02_THE_PEOPLE**
    *   **Prompt**: Montage sequence. First, a bustling, colorful African market in Bamako, people smiling, vibrant fabrics, shallow depth of field isolating faces. Smooth transition to a rural village school with laughing children, warm sunlight. Then, color grading shifts to cold/desaturated, showing an empty, abandoned market with wind blowing dust. Final shot is a long column of refugees walking across a dusty horizon at sunset, silhouetted against a dark orange sky. Cinematic, emotional, anamorphic lens flares, film grain, slow motion.

*   **SEQ_03_THE_CRISIS**
    *   **Prompt**: A chaotic, fast-paced sequence. Abstract map of West Africa on a digital screen, red zones spreading like ink on paper. Interposed with quick flashes of news footage style clips: a military convoy moving fast, a gavel striking a desk, a drought-stricken field. Creates a sense of urgency and overwhelming pressure. Gritty realism mixed with high-tech graphics. High contrast, jarring cuts, muted colors with piercing red alerts. 4k resolution.

*   **SEQ_04_THE_MOMENT**
    *   **Prompt**: Slow tracking shot moving through the modern corridors of the African Union headquarters in Addis Ababa. Professional diplomats in suits discussing intensely in hallways, shallow depth of field. The camera pushes forward towards a pair of large, heavy wooden doors. The doors slowly creak open revealing a bright light from within. First-person perspective. Afro-Futurist political thriller aesthetic. Glossy, high stakes, cool interior lighting matching warm exterior light.

*   **SEQ_05_THE_CHARGE**
    *   **Prompt**: A high-tech digital holographic map of the Sahel region initiates in a darkened command room. The map zooms in, showing terrain details, borders, and glowing hotspot markers in Mali and Burkina Faso. The interface pulses with "Incoming Alert" graphics. Sci-fi meets tactical military aesthetic, deep blue and cyan bioluminescent UI colors contrasted with gold terrain data. 4k motion graphics loop, macro lens details on the pixels.

### 1.2 Narrative Cutscenes & Backgrounds

*   **VID_TUTORIAL_INTRO (Chief of Staff)**
    *   **Prompt**: Medium shot of a professional African man, aged 40s, wearing a sharp suit and an earpiece, standing in a high-tech command center. He is looking directly at the camera and speaking (lip sync ready). Behind him, blurred screens show maps and data with teal and blue bokeh. Professional cinematic lighting, key light on face, rim light from screens. Anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, 4k video portrait.

*   **VID_LOOP_SITUATION_ROOM**
    *   **Prompt**: Wide shot of a modern, high-tech Situation Room in an African capital. Large glass windows overlook a city skyline at twilight with purple and orange gradients. Inside, large screens display maps with cool blue tactical overlays. Ambient movement of dust motes in light beams, screens flickering, ceiling fans rotating slowly. Cinemagraph style, calm but tense atmosphere. High contrast, teal and orange color grading.

*   **VID_LOOP_DESERT_CAMP**
    *   **Prompt**: A view of a Tuareg camp in the desert at night. A small fire crackles in the foreground providing warm orange light. Tents are illuminated from within with soft glow. The Milky Way is clearly visible in the sky above (cool blues/purples). Gentle wind movement in the fabric of the tents. Photorealistic, peaceful, atmospheric loop. National Geographic night photography style, grain, high dynamic range.

## 2. Midjourney Prompts (Visual Assets)

*   Standard Aspect Ratio: `--ar 16:9` (for backgrounds/cinematics)
*   Portrait Aspect Ratio: `--ar 2:3` (for character profiles)
*   Style: `--v 6.0` (or latest model)
