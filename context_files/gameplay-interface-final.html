Mission Brief

Operation Sahel Stabilization - Act 1

Player Role
Special Envoy for Sahel Stabilization
You serve as the African Union's lead crisis negotiator and operational strategist in the Sahel. Your mandate is to coordinate regional partners, allocate limited resources, and shape outcomes that will be used to grade mission performance across security, humanitarian access, and political legitimacy.

Situation Overview

The Sahel region faces an unprecedented convergence of security, governance, and humanitarian crises. Jihadist insurgencies have expanded across Mali, Burkina Faso, and Niger, displacing millions and destabilizing national governments. Military juntas have seized power in multiple countries, expelled Western forces, and invited Russian Wagner Group contractors as alternative security partners.

As the African Union's Special Envoy for Sahel Stabilization, you must navigate this complex landscape to restore stability, protect civilian populations, and preserve African-led solutions to regional challenges. Your decisions will shape the future of the Sahel and test the AU's capacity for effective crisis response.

Primary Objectives

01
Stabilize Critical Zones
Reduce insurgency levels in high-threat zones across Mali, Burkina Faso, and Niger. Prevent the collapse of urban centers under siege and protect civilian populations from escalating violence.
02
Build Regional Cooperation
Strengthen coordination between ECOWAS, national governments, and local actors. Navigate the political tensions created by military juntas while maintaining pressure for democratic transitions.
03
Counter External Interference
Address the expanding presence of Wagner Group contractors and other external actors who undermine African-led security frameworks. Offer credible alternatives that respect sovereignty while advancing regional stability.
04
Protect Civilian Populations
Ensure humanitarian access to displaced populations, prevent mass atrocities, and address the root causes of conflict including governance failures, climate impacts, and economic marginalization.

Victory Conditions

To successfully complete your mission within the 36-month timeframe, you must achieve the following measurable outcomes:

✓
Regional Stability ≥ 70
Achieve and maintain a regional stability score of at least 70/100. This represents sustainable security conditions across the Sahel, with functioning governance structures and reduced violence.
✓
Insurgency Level ≤ 35
Reduce the regional insurgency level to 35/100 or below. This indicates that jihadist groups no longer pose an existential threat to national governments or civilian populations.
✓
Civilian Support ≥ 65
Build civilian support for stabilization efforts to at least 65/100. Local populations must trust that peace processes serve their interests and address root causes of conflict.
✓
No Critical Zones
Ensure no zones remain at "Critical" threat level by mission end. All territories should be stabilized to "Moderate" or "Low" threat status, with functioning local governance.

Note: You must achieve ALL four victory conditions simultaneously before time expires. Partial success will not prevent the region from sliding back into chaos. The AU's credibility—and the future of African-led security frameworks—depends on your complete success.

Strategic Guidance

Balance Multiple Priorities: You cannot solve every crisis simultaneously. Focus your limited actions on high-impact interventions that create cascading positive effects across multiple objectives.

Engage Key Actors: Success requires building relationships with military leaders, insurgent groups, regional organizations, and local communities. Each actor has distinct interests and leverage points—understand them before engaging.

Monitor Intelligence: The Intelligence Feed provides critical real-time information about emerging threats and opportunities. Review reports carefully to inform your strategic decisions.

Manage Resources: Your budget, political capital, and personnel are finite. Strategic investments in capacity building and diplomatic engagement often yield better long-term results than reactive crisis responses.

Strategic Hint

Start with Intelligence Gathering: Before taking major actions, investigate zones to understand local dynamics. The most effective interventions are those informed by ground-level intelligence. Consider deploying reconnaissance to critical zones in Mali and Burkina Faso to identify where your initial actions will have maximum impact.

Build Relationships Early: Engaging with key actors like Col. Goïta, ECOWAS leadership, and local community representatives early in Act 1 creates diplomatic channels you'll need later. Don't wait for crises to escalate before establishing these connections.
