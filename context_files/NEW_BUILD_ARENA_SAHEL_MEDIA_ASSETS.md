# MEDIA ASSETS LIST: African Mandate - Sahel Arena

This document details the required media assets (Video, Audio, Visual, UI) for the production of *African Mandate: Sahel Arena*, based on the Build Description, Narrative Book, and Schema.

## 1. Video & Cinematic Assets

### 1.1 Opening Sequence (Pre-Title)
**Style:** Documentary-meets-cinematic. Real satellite imagery, stylized 3D map animations, archival footage.
*   **SEQ_01_THE_LAND**: 
    *   *Visuals*: Aerial Sahara, transition to Sahel savanna, verdant farmland. Time-lapse of desertification (green turning to brown/yellow), shrinking lakes.
    *   *Length*: ~20 sec.
*   **SEQ_02_THE_PEOPLE**: 
    *   *Visuals*: Montage of markets, schools, farmers, herders. Transition to darkening scenes, empty markets, refugee columns.
    *   *Length*: ~25 sec.
*   **SEQ_03_THE_CRISIS**: 
    *   *Visuals*: Rapid news headlines (EN/FR) flashing. Animated map with red zones spreading. Split screen of simultaneous crises (gunfire, coups, drought).
    *   *Length*: ~35 sec.
*   **SEQ_04_THE_MOMENT**: 
    *   *Visuals*: AU & ECOWAS HQs (real footage), frustrated diplomats. Camera moving through corridors to closed chamber doors. Doors opening (Player POV).
    *   *Length*: ~25 sec.
*   **SEQ_05_THE_CHARGE**: 
    *   *Visuals*: Screen filled with AU/ECOWAS emblems. Map of Sahel becoming interactive game interface. Impulse/Pulse on hotspots.
    *   *Length*: ~20 sec.

### 1.2 Narrative Cutscenes (In-Game)
*   **CS_OPENING_BRIEFING**:
    *   *Setting*: Situation Room.
    *   *Content*: Chief of Staff briefing on 3 initial crises (Goundam, Dori, Diffa). "Situation Room" interface loading.
*   **CS_ACT_TRANSITION_1_TO_2**:
    *   *Setting*: Montage of early results.
    *   *Content*: Visual summary of Act 1 outcomes leading into Coalition Building phase.
*   **CS_ECON_INITIATIVE_PROPOSAL** (Act 4, Event 4-2):
    *   *Setting*: Video conference.
    *   *Content*: African Development Bank President proposing "Sahel Economic Integration Initiative".
*   **CS_HUMANITARIAN_CRISIS** (Act 4, Event 4-3):
    *   *Setting*: Emergency Coordination Meeting.
    *   *Content*: UN Coordinator & MSF Rep detailing the "lost generation" and systemic collapse.
*   **CS_CLIMATE_BRIEF** (Act 4, Event 4-4):
    *   *Setting*: Video Conference.
    *   *Content*: Climate Scientists presenting data on Lake Chad collapse and irreversible trends.
*   **CS_MANDATE_REVIEW** (Act 4 Conclusion):
    *   *Setting*: AU Peace & Security Council Chamber.
    *   *Content*: Annual review, "Are we winning?" speech by Chairperson. Variant player reports (High/Mod/Low performance).
*   **CS_ACT_5_OPENING**:
    *   *Setting*: Dawn over Sahel (Aerial).
    *   *Content*: Narrator reflection on "What happens when you leave?". Transition to final strategy session.
*   **CS_SUCCESSION_DEBATE** (Act 5, Event 5-1):
    *   *Setting*: AU Council Special Session.
    *   *Content*: Debate on Permanent Envoy Office vs. Return to States vs. G5/AES Integration.
*   **CS_ENDING_VARIANTS**:
    *   *Content*: Multiple stylized cinematics for different endings (e.g., "The Institutional Victory", "The Fragile Peace", "The Collapse").

### 1.3 Tutorial & Onboarding
*   **VID_TUTORIAL_INTRO**: Video modal welcoming the Special Envoy.
*   **VID_LOOP_BACKGROUNDS**: 
    *   Situation Room Ambience (screens flickering, staff moving).
    *   Map Idle States (clouds passing, threat pulses).

## 2. Audio Assets

### 2.1 Music Score
*   **MUS_THEME_MAIN**:
    *   *Description*: Serious, urgent, orchestral mixed with African percussion and kora. Builds from ominous drone to driving rhythm.
*   **MUS_AMBIENT_SAFE**:
    *   *Description*: Calm, contemplative, hopeful. For stable regions and menus.
*   **MUS_AMBIENT_TENSION**:
    *   *Description*: Low frequency drone, sparse percussion. For crisis management and unstable regions.
*   **MUS_ABMIENT_CRITICAL**:
    *   *Description*: Fast-tempo, chaotic, dissonant. For combat/high-threat events.
*   **MUS_THEME_HOPE/RECOVERY**:
    *   *Description*: Uplifting, major key, more melodic. Used when metrics improve or positive cutscenes play.
*   **MUS_STINGER_VICTORY**: Short cue for successful operation.
*   **MUS_STINGER_DEFEAT**: Short cue for failed operation or critical alert.
*   **MUS_SFX_UI**:
    *   Click_Light, Click_Heavy, Hover_Digital, Notification_Alert, Map_Zoom_In/Out, Resource_Spend.

### 2.2 Voice-Over (VO)
*   **VO_NARRATOR**:
    *   *Character*: Warm, authoritative, Sahelian accent (EN/FR).
    *   *Lines*: Opening sequence narration, Act transitions.
*   **VO_PLAYER_INTERNAL**:
    *   *Character*: The Special Envoy (Gender/Tone options?). Inner monologues reflecting on moral dilemmas.
    *   *Lines*: Opening monologue ("My grandmother used to say..."), Act 4 Reflections ("Is it pragmatism or numbness?").
*   **VO_CHIEF_OF_STAFF**:
    *   *Character*: Professional, efficient, Deputy.
    *   *Lines*: Daily briefings, urgent alerts, tutorial guidance.
*   **VO_AU_CHAIRPERSON**:
    *   *Character*: Senior statesman/woman, grave, demanding.
    *   *Lines*: Mandate charge, annual reviews.
*   **VO_ACTOR_LEADERS**:
    *   Short barks/greeting lines for:
        *   Col. Assimi Goïta (Mali)
        *   Capt. Ibrahim Traoré (Burkina Faso)
        *   Gen. Tiani (Niger)
        *   JNIM Leader
        *   ADB President
        *   UN Coordinator
        *   Climate Scientist

### 2.3 Environmental Audio
*   **AMB_MARKET**: Crowded market sounds, distant chatter, animals.
*   **AMB_OFFICE**: Air conditioning hum, distant phones, paper shuffling.
*   **AMB_DESERT_WIND**: High whistling wind, sand movement.
*   **AMB_CONFLICT**: Distant explosions, sirens, helicopter rotors.

## 3. Visual Assets (2D & UI)

### 3.1 Character Portraits (Art Style: Realistic Digital Painting or High-Res Photo-bash)
*   **Main Cast**:
    *   **Special Envoy (Player)**: Generic silhouette or multiple selectable avatars.
    *   **Chief of Staff**: Professional attire, earpiece/tablet.
    *   **AU Chairperson**: Formal traditional or western business wear.
    *   **Intelligence Chief**: Military/Intel uniform.
    *   **Economic Advisor**: Business formal.
*   **State Actors (Based on real likenesses where appropriate/safe, or stylized equivalents)**:
    *   **Mali Junta Leader**: Military fatigues, beret.
    *   **Burkina Junta Leader**: Military fatigues, distinctive red beret.
    *   **Niger Leader**: Military uniform.
    *   **Chad Leader**: Military general attire.
*   **Non-State Actors**:
    *   **JNIM Commander**: Traditional Tuareg/religious dress, face covering options.
    *   **ISGS Commander**: Field combat attire.
    *   **Community Leaders**: Traditional robes (Boubou), varying by ethnicity (Tuareg, Fulani, Dogon).
*   **External Envoys**:
    *   **French/US/EU Diplomats**: Western suits.
    *   **Wagner Representative**: Mercenary tactical gear/informal mix.

### 3.2 Environments / Backgrounds
*   **BG_SITUATION_ROOM**:
    *   High-tech African command center. Screens showing maps/data. Glass walls overlooking a Sahelian capital skyline.
    *   *Variants*: Day, Night, Crisis Mode (Red lighting).
*   **BG_AU_COUNCIL**:
    *   Formal circular chamber, wood paneling, flags of member states.
*   **BG_FIELD_LOCATIONS** (Matte paintings/Photos):
    *   **Northern Mali**: Desert dunes, Timbuktu architecture.
    *   **Central Mali**: River Niger wetlands, villages.
    *   **Eastern Burkina**: Arid scrubland, gold mining camps.
    *   **Lake Chad**: Marshlands, water receding visuals.
    *   **IDP Camps**: Tents, crowds, dust.
    *   **Urban Capitals**: Bamako/Ouagadougou street scenes, traffic.

### 3.3 UI Elements & Icons
*   **Maps**:
    *   **Base Map**: Satellite/Topographic hybrid of Sahel.
    *   **Overlays**: Heatmaps (Insurgency Red, Stability Blue), Borders (Porous vs Secure).
    *   **Icons**:
        *   *Units*: Soldier, Aid Truck, Drone, Spy/Intel.
        *   *Events*: Explosion (Attack), Handshake (Diplomacy), Speaker (Protest), Drop/Crate (Aid).
        *   *Locations*: City (Capital/Provincial), Mine (Gold/Uranium), Oil Well, Lake, Ruins (Heritage).
*   **Dashboard**:
    *   **Gauges/Meters**: Circular or Bar designs for: Stability, Insurgency, Legitimacy, Support, Synergy.
    *   **Resource Icons**: Budget ($), Personnel (Meeple), Pol. Capital (Gavel/Star), Intel (Eye/File).
*   **Logos/Emblems**:
    *   AU, ECOWAS, AES (Alliance of Sahel States), United Nations, G5 Sahel.
    *   Fictional logos for specific in-game NGOs or factions.

### 3.4 Event Illustrations (Still Art for Pop-ups)
*   **EVT_COUP**: Soldiers seizing a radio station or palace.
*   **EVT_DROUGHT**: Dried cracked earth, cattle carcasses.
*   **EVT_PROTEST**: Crowds with placards in city streets.
*   **EVT_NEGOTIATION**: Silhouette of figures at a table in a tent.
*   **EVT_COMBAT**: Dust cloud, distant vehicles, flashes.
*   **EVT_RELIEF**: Food distribution, sacks of grain.

## 4. 3D & Animation Assets

### 4.1 Map Animations
*   **ANIM_MAP_SPREAD**: Red "infection" spreading effect for insurgency.
*   **ANIM_MAP_HEAL**: Blue/Green "stabilization" wave effect.
*   **ANIM_TRAVEL**: Dotted lines tracing movement of envoys or troops.
*   **ANIM_WEATHER**: Cloud layers, sandstorm particle effects over specific regions.

### 4.2 UI Animations
*   **UI_TRANSITION_GLITCH**: Digital distortion effect for "Crisis" alerts.
*   **UI_LOADING_HOLO**: Holographic projection style loading for the Situation Room.
*   **UI_METRIC_UPDATE**: Smooth filling/draining animations for resource bars.
