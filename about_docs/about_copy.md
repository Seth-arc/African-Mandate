
African Mandate: Sahel Arena plunges you into the heart of West Africa's Sahel crisis as an African Union Special Envoy. Over 20 turns spanning 48 months, stabilize a region gripped by insurgencies, coups, climate collapse, and fractured alliances.

Crisis Core
The Sahel spans Mali, Burkina Faso, Niger, Chad, and Mauritania, with 30 interactive zones tracking threats, populations, and ethnic tensions. Face dynamic events like insurgent surges, corruption scandals, and donor pullouts that force brutal tradeoffs.
​

Your Mandate
Wield three actions per turn across security patrols, diplomacy, governance reforms, aid drops, intel ops, and community mediation. Scarce resources—budget, political capital, personnel—demand ruthless prioritization amid evolving actor relationships.
​

Every Choice Has a Cost
Balance five metrics: stability, insurgency, civilian support, global legitimacy, regional synergy. No victory is clean; choose security-first aggression, governance builds, coalition plays, or community trust—and unlock one of five endings from triumph to mandate collapse.
​

Powered by Reality
Browser-based with React mapping, AI-driven insurgents, and narrative acts that immerse without illusions. Play as guest or authenticated for saves; experience the asymmetry where partial success is the true win.
